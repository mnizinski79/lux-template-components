Real padding (40/28/28/28 desktop, 28/20/20/20 mobile), real gaps (16/36 desktop, 12/24 mobile), real fonts (Canela title 28px/44px desktop · 22px/32px mobile, IBM Plex Sans description/button, Engravers Gothic meta). No line-clamp exists in the real component — content that's too tall gets pushed up and clipped at the card's top edge, since the frost box is bottom-anchored and hugs its own height.

Target content budget (Julia/Ryan/Francisco): 4:5 — header up to 2 lines, description up to 3-line clamp  ·  1:1 — header capped at 1 line, description capped at 2-line clamp. Flags below check both: whether the real component visually clips this content (pushed off the card top), and separately whether it's within the target budget (which is stricter, meant to keep content away from the clip point entirely).
