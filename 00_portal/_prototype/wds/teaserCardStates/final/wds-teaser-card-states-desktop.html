What to check

This is a live, interactive comparison — trigger each state directly on the card instead of judging from static Figma frames. The two behaviors below are what's actually under review for whether the base teaserCard ships animated in v1.

  • Hover — image scales to 1.06x within the fixed card bounds, clipped so nothing shifts around it. Scrim, text, and link stay put; this signals the whole card is a single interactive target, not just the link.
  • Focus — Tab lands on the card first (its own visible ring), then Tab again moves to the nested link, which has its own distinct focus treatment. Two separate, sequential tab stops.
  • Active — an 18% opacity black overlay washes across the full image on click/tap. It's momentary and reverts to Default as soon as the click releases — no layout or text change.
